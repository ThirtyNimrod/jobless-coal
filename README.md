# Prac-and-Notes

Welcome to the **Prac-and-Notes** repository, where Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA) meet the art of organized chaos! 🎉

## What's the Deal?
This repository is your one-stop shop for:
- **Problem Descriptions**: Straight from LeetCode, because why reinvent the wheel?
- **Solutions**: Multiple approaches, because we like options (and overthinking).
- **Explanations**: Progressive breakdowns, because brute force is just the beginning.
- **Concept Lessons**: Extracted wisdom, because every problem teaches something (even if it's just patience).

## Directory Tour 🚀
- **1.questions/**: Problem statements live here. Think of it as the "What?" section.
- **2.answers/**: Solutions galore! Python scripts with comments that (hopefully) make sense.
- **3.understand/**: Progressive explanations. From "Huh?" to "Aha!" in one file.
- **study/**: Concept lessons. The "Why" behind the "What" and "How."
- **index.md**: The grand index. Your map to navigate this labyrinth of knowledge.

## Why This Exists 🤔
Because solving problems is fun, but documenting them is an art. And we believe in sharing the art (and the pain) of learning DSA.

## How to Use This Treasure Trove 🗺️
1. Pick a problem from `1.questions/`.
2. Check out the solutions in `2.answers/`.
3. Dive into the explanations in `3.understand/`.
4. Level up with the lessons in `study/`.
5. Update `index.md` to keep the chaos organized.

## Warning ⚠️
Side effects of using this repo may include:
- Sudden bursts of enlightenment.
- Overuse of the two-pointer technique.
- An irrational love for XOR operations.

Enjoy the journey, and may your debugging sessions be short and your solutions optimal! 🚀
